# Why I Love Modern Family
> "A family is a family, no matter how different it looks."
Modern Family is an American **mockumentary sitcom** that follows three related families living in the same city. I like it because it's *funny*, warm, and honest about family life.[^1]
[^1]: The show aired on ABC from 2009 to 2020.

## Meet the Families
- **The Pritchetts:** Jay, Gloria, Manny, and Joe
- **The Dunphys:** Phil, Claire, Haley, Alex, and Luke
- **The Pritchett-Tuckers:** Mitchell, Cameron, and Lily

## Quick Facts
| Family | Known For |
| ------ | --------- |
| Pritchetts | Jay's grumpy humor and Gloria's big personality |
| Dunphys | Phil's dad jokes and everyday chaos |
| Pritchett-Tuckers | Cam and Mitchell's dramatic parenting moments |

## My Top 3 Reasons to Watch
1. The characters feel like real people.
2. The talking-head interviews make every joke funnier.
3. Every episode has something to laugh about and something to learn.
